What is PPC Advertising?

PPC (Pay-Per-Click) advertising is a digital marketing strategy where businesses pay only when someone clicks on their ads. These advertisements appear on search engines, websites, and social media platforms.

  • Google Ads
  • Bing Ads
  • Facebook Ads
  • Instagram Ads
  • YouTube Advertising
  • LinkedIn Ads
